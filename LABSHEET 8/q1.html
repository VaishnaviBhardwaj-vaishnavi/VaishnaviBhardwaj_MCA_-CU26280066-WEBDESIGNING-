The Golden Crust - Project Specification

1. Client Information

Client Name: The Golden Crust

Business Type: Artisan Bakery

2. Project Objective

To develop a responsive bakery website where customers can explore products and book orders.

3. Target Audience

Students, families and working professionals.

4. Website Features

  • Home Page
  • About Us
  • Products and Services
  • Customer Reviews
  • Booking Form
  • Contact Information

5. Technologies

HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript.

6. Expected Result

A responsive and user-friendly bakery website.
